Privacy Policy

Slime Story: Farm & Craft — Rigamura Studios
Last updated: draft of 2026-11-12 (version 2026-08-06-draft1)

1. Who controls your data

Rigamura Studios is the controller of the personal data processed in this game, under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018) and, where applicable, other data protection laws. Because we are a small studio, we have not formally appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO). Instead, we keep an active contact channel for any request about your data: rigamurastudios@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

This table reflects exactly what the game collects and stores today, and nothing else:

DataWhat it is
Anonymous player IDAn opaque identifier generated by Unity Gaming Services on first launch. It is not your real name or email.
Google Play Games link (optional)If you choose to link your Google account, we keep only the confirmation of the link, never your email or your real Google name.
Player nameThe nickname you choose to type in your Profile, stored on your device and synced in your save.
Game progress dataLevel, items, farm, achievements: the save itself.
Public card and friendsIf you use the social features, we publish a card with your nickname, level, title, slime color and accessories, amount of Gold and Essence, and the Tower floor you are on. It is visible to your friends and in the rankings among friends (and you see your friends' cards). We also store your friend code, your friend list, and the gifts sent and received.
Purchased product IDsWhich items you bought inside the app. We never receive payment or card data, which stays with Google. When the game starts, it may query Google Play to recover purchases your account already made (for example, on a new device).
Support and error logA short technical history tied to your player ID, used only for support and game stability: errors that occurred (technical message, how many times, game version), purchase results and support events.
Currency and XP historyA record of recent Gold, Essence and XP movements (amount, where in the game it happened and the balance afterwards), used to investigate reports such as "I lost coins" and game balance problems.
Technical device informationDevice model, Android version, amount of memory (RAM), game version, platform type and date/time of the last connection. We do not collect IMEI, serial number, location or the list of installed apps.
Age bracket + consentIn the Age Gate we compute your age bracket (not the date of birth itself, which is discarded) and store when you accepted the Terms.
Public farm (snapshot)A compact summary of your farm's state (only which plots are ready to harvest), visible to anyone who visits as a friend.
Security historyTo detect fraud and problems with purchases and refunds, we keep for up to 90 days a technical history tied to your player ID, with level, XP, Gold, Essence, purchased items, date of first launch, the save's clock and purchase results. It does not include your name or device information, and it is kept on a studio computer, not on publicly accessible servers. We also record in your save when a purchase in the save is not confirmed by Google Play.
Account restrictionIf an account is restricted for abuse or fraud (for example, exploiting bugs or abusing refunds), we store the restriction flag, the reason and the date, tied to your player ID. The restriction blocks the social features and the Store; solo play keeps working.
Support contactIf you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you tell us in the message (including your support code and your Google Play order number, if you send them). We use it only to answer you and solve your case.
Ads (optional)The game offers optional ads, in exchange for rewards, provided by Unity LevelPlay and partner ad networks. These services may collect on their own the device's advertising identifier and ad interaction data, under their own policies. For players aged 13 to 17, the game requests child-directed treatment for ads (no personalization and no use of the advertising identifier for targeting). For everyone, we ask the networks for a content rating ceiling suitable for teens. Players who buy the remove-ads option no longer see ads.
Remote settingsThe game fetches settings (feature switches) from Unity Remote Config. We send no personal data of yours in that request beyond the service's technical identifier.
Slime photosPhotos you take in the Photo Studio are generated on your device. They leave it only if you choose to share them or save them to your gallery.

3. Why we collect it

  • To make the game work and save your progress between sessions;
  • To sync your progress across different devices;
  • To provide social features (friends, farm visits);
  • To process and deliver in-app purchases;
  • To support you when you report a problem;
  • To keep the game stable: identify errors and performance problems on specific versions or devices;
  • To prevent fraud and abuse: detect improper save changes, items obtained without purchase and abusive use of refunds, and restrict accounts when necessary;
  • To offer optional ads in exchange for rewards;
  • To comply with legal obligations related to the minimum age of users.

For support, game stability and fraud prevention, the legal basis is the studio's legitimate interest (LGPD, Art. 7, IX), limited to what is necessary. The technical information we collect on our own initiative (errors, currency history, security history and device data) is not used for advertising or to build a player profile. Fraud alerts are only a lead: the analysis and any account restriction are made by a member of our team, not automatically.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We use the following third-party services, each acting as a processor of the data they handle on our behalf:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication + Cloud Save): hosts your player ID and your progress data;
  • Google Play Games Services: used only if you choose to link your account;
  • Google Play Billing: processes in-app purchases and tells the game which purchases your account already made;
  • Unity LevelPlay and partner ad networks: show the optional ads and may process the device's advertising identifier, under their own policies;
  • Unity Cloud Code and Remote Config: run server functions (friends, gifts, server clock) and deliver remote game settings.

Progress data, the public card and the security history are our responsibility. The security history is kept on a studio computer and consulted only by our team.

5. Your rights (LGPD, Art. 18)

As the data subject, you have the right to:

RightHow to exercise it
Access and portabilitySettings → Download my data, inside the game.
CorrectionYour player name can be edited directly in the Profile. For other data, contact support.
DeletionSettings → Delete my account, inside the game, or through our web deletion channel, even without the app installed. Deletion also erases your security history and your public card.
Information about processingYou can ask, through the contact email, which data of yours we hold, what we use it for and who we share it with (especially about ads, purchases and account restrictions).
Review of an account restrictionIf your account was restricted and you think it was a mistake, write to support (Settings → Support) with your support code. A member of the team reviews the case.
Withdrawal of consentEquivalent to deleting your account, since the game collects the minimum needed to work.

You may also contact the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) if you are not satisfied with our answer.

6. Minimum age policy

On first launch, we ask for your date of birth (never stored; only the resulting age bracket is kept). Players under 13 cannot go past that screen. We deliberately collect no data from children under 13 beyond what is needed to enforce that block.

7. Data retention

Your data is stored while your account exists. When you delete your account, we erase your saved progress, identity and associated data from our systems (see section 5). The technical histories (errors, currency and XP) keep only the most recent events: older ones are discarded automatically, and everything is erased together with the account. The security history is kept for up to 90 days and automatically erased after that (or earlier, if you delete your account). The account restriction flag, when one exists, stays while the account exists. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to solve the case and to meet legal obligations.

8. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect data: access to cloud data is made only by our team, with our own credentials; account restriction flags can only be written by the server; and the security history is kept on a studio computer with access restricted to the team. No system is 100% secure. If an incident occurs that may cause you a relevant risk, we will notify you and the ANPD, as required by the LGPD.

9. International transfers

Unity Gaming Services and Google Play run global infrastructure. Your data may be processed on servers outside Brazil, always under the data protection terms of those providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced inside the game.
