Delete my data

Slime Story: Farm & Craft — Rigamura Studios

If you still have the game installed, the fastest way is Settings → Delete my account inside the app. This page is for when you no longer have the game installed, or prefer to request deletion without opening the app.

How to request deletion through the web

Send an email to rigamurastudios@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request, including:

  • Your support code, if you have it (see below how to find it);
  • If you do not have the code, any information that helps identify your account (the player name used in the game, device, approximate date you played).
Send the request email

How to find your support code

Inside the game: Settings → Support. The code is shown on screen and can be copied with one tap. It is the unique identifier of your account, with no personal information in it.

What is erased

When we process your request, we erase:

  • Your player identity in Unity Gaming Services;
  • All your progress saved in the cloud (farm, items, achievements, recorded purchases);
  • Your support history tied to the account;
  • Your security history and your public card.

Data that exists only locally on your own device (the save of the installed game) cannot be erased remotely by us. To remove it, simply uninstall the app from your device.

Timeframe

This channel is still processed manually by our team (as is our Support in general). We will confirm receipt and carry out the deletion as soon as possible. If you do not get an answer within a few business days, feel free to send the request again.
