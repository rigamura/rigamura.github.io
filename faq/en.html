Getting started

How do I get started?

The tutorial walks you through the first steps: tap an empty plot to plant, water it so it grows faster, tap to harvest, open the Cauldron and trade Essence for Gold. With Gold you buy more plots and seeds. You can replay the lesson anytime with Replay tutorial in Settings.

How do I replay the tutorial?

In Settings, tap Replay tutorial. In the replay the slime only shows and explains: you do not spend Essence, Gold or seeds to follow along. On the same screen, the Tutorial option turns the lessons on or off.

What is Slime Story: Farm & Craft?

A cozy idle farming game. You plant, harvest, trade what you grow for coins, expand your farm and grow together with your slime. Plants keep growing even while the game is closed, so you can play a little every day or for long sessions.

Who is the Wizard Slime and what does it do?

Your slime, your farm partner. It wanders around the clearing, reacts when you tap it and, with the right skills, helps by harvesting and planting on its own (slower than you on purpose: tapping is still faster). It can also go study at the University.

How do I take care of my slime?

In Care for your slime there are three bars that drop slowly over time (more slowly while the game is closed). Feeding: give it a dish cooked at the Cauldron. Affection: tap the slime on the farm. Rest: the Sleep button sends the slime home for a nap (tap the moon icon to wake it early). The better the care, the faster it walks, harvests and plants.

What is the record player for and how do I get discs?

Each disc is one of the game's songs. Open the record player, tap a disc to listen and choose Shuffle or Repeat. While a disc plays, your slime slowly gains Affection. Discs you do not have yet show how to get them; seasonal ones can only be earned during their season.

Tips for the early game Tip

1) Follow the tutorial and open the Cauldron as soon as it unlocks. 2) Trade Essence for Gold and use Gold for plots and seeds. 3) Always plant the best available seed. 4) At level 3 Light Transmutation unlocks: start transmuting in batches. 5) When you return to the game, collect the welcome panel. 6) Complete the easy achievements, which give lots of XP early on.

Tips for the mid game Tip

Study at the University to automate the farm. Transmute in batches and complete the collectibles to unlock Album memories and later earn materials for Crafting. Use the mini games as extra income. Climb the Tower and upgrade its slots, which is a great place to spend Gold. Check whether a season is active so you do not miss its exclusive items.

Tips for advanced players Tip

Keep the farm full of the best plants and let automatic harvesting do its work. Use bigger transmutations for rare materials and to finish the Album. Build matching cosmetic sets and take photos. Compare your Tower floor and level with friends in the Ranking and plan what to upgrade next.

Glossary: what do the game's terms mean?

Essence: the currency the farm produces. Gold: the currency you get by trading Essence. Transmutation: pouring Essence into the Cauldron to get materials and rare items. Fires (Light, Arcane, Mythic): the Cauldron's levels. Material: an item used to Craft. Collectible: a rare item that feeds the Album. Consumable: a potion or scroll for temporary use. Memory Fragment: unlocks an Album memory. Season Fragment: a free currency to make event items. Vigor: your slime's health in the Tower. Guardian: the Tower boss every 10 floors. Upgrade: raising an accessory slot's level. Ranking: the friends leaderboard.

Farm and currencies

Can I plant different seeds? How do I remove a plant?

Yes. New plants unlock as you level up: Rotcar, Tatopo, Matoto, Paragusas, Tucelet, Dishra, Mupkin, Beetro and Applepine, plus the special Golden Seed. Better plants give more Essence but take longer. Pick the seed in the bag. To pull a plant out, hold the plot (the seed is lost).

How do I unlock more plots?

Tap a locked plot next to your land and confirm the purchase with Gold. How many plots you can own also depends on your level: if you see “Requires level X”, level up first. The farm has 64 plots in total.

What are Essence, Gold and Seeds for?

Essence is what the farm produces when you harvest. In the Cauldron you trade Essence for Gold (the rate is shown on screen). Gold buys plots and seeds and pays for studying, upgrading and crafting. Seeds are what you plant: they live in the seed bag and are bought with Gold. The game blocks spending that would leave you unable to buy seeds.

What happens when I close the game?

Plants keep growing and your progress stays saved on the device. When you come back, the welcome panel shows what is ready to collect. You can collect everything, harvest by hand, or watch an optional ad to collect double (there is a daily limit). With Remove Ads you collect double right away, with no video.

How do I level up?

You earn XP by harvesting, unlocking plots, completing achievements (each achievement gives a share of a level) and finishing Daily Missions. Leveling up unlocks better plants, more plots, stronger transmutations and other parts of the game.

I ran out of seeds. Now what?

Tap the seed bag and buy more with Gold. If you are short on Gold, trade Essence for Gold at the Cauldron. Tip: keep a little Gold saved to restock. With the planting modes from the University, the slime moves on to another seed when one runs out.

Why do I see "Keep a reserve for seeds!"?

It is a safety net: the game will not let you spend so much that you end up with no seeds and no way to buy more, which would stall your farm. The wording changes a little from screen to screen. Gather a bit more Essence or Gold and try again.

How does the seed shop work (buying and selling)?

Tap the seed bag to open it. On the Buy tab you pick the seed and the amount and pay with Gold. On the Sell tab you trade leftover seeds for Gold (you only get back part of the buy price) or just discard them. Each seed shows the level at which it unlocks.

How does the slime choose which seed to plant on its own?

By default the slime plants only the seed you selected on the Seeds screen; if it runs out, it stops planting. At the University you can study three upgrades that unlock other modes: Thrifty Planting (cheapest first), Varied Planting (one of each kind, taking turns) and Premium Planting (most expensive first). After studying, choose the mode with the Planting button on the Seeds screen. In those modes the slime keeps planting while there is any seed left. The Golden Seed never enters the automatic modes.

Can I skip a plant's growing time?

Yes, by watching an optional ad: tap a plant that is still growing and confirm. There is a daily limit. With Remove Ads the button becomes Grow now, with no video (the daily limit still applies). Watering and potions also speed up growth.

What is the watering can for?

A watered plot grows faster until harvest. Tap the watering can button to turn on watering mode, then tap growing plots. Water runs out: tap the pond to refill for free. To leave, tap Stop Watering or the watering can again. At the University you can raise the can's capacity and learn Gardening, which makes the slime water on its own. Nothing bad happens if you do not water.

What are the weeds?

From level 8, a weed sprouts in the clearing from time to time, outside the plots. Tap it to pull it out and get a small reward (a little Gold and Essence, and sometimes seeds). Ignoring them does no harm. With the Weeding skill from the University, the slime pulls them on its own and you still get the reward.

What are the parachutes that land in the clearing?

Every now and then, while you play, a parachute pack lands somewhere in the clearing and stays on the ground for a few minutes. Tap it to get a random reward. Time only counts while the game is open, and there is a limit per part of the day.

What are the Daily Missions?

They unlock at level 2. Every day you get 3 simple missions (plant, harvest, pet your slime and others), each with its own reward. Completing all 3 opens the Day's Chest and gives you the Day's Blessing, a temporary bonus to growth and double harvest. You can swap a mission for another one by paying Gold (its progress is lost, and each swap costs more until the next day). The forest residents' Orders are on the same screen.

Cauldron

What is the Cauldron and what can I do with it?

Tap it on the farm to open it. The Cauldron has four functions: Convert (trades Essence for Gold), Transmute (you spend Essence and get materials and rare items), Cook (makes dishes to feed your slime) and Alchemy (makes potions and Tower items). The INFO icon on each tab explains the details.

How do Transmutations work?

You choose how many transmutations to make at once (1 to 50) and spend Essence. Each transmutation gives 1 guaranteed material and a chance of a bonus rare item. Light Transmutation unlocks at level 3, Arcane Flame at 10 and Mythic Fire at 20; bigger fires give better materials. When a transmutation misses the bonus, you can sometimes watch an optional ad to try again.

Where do I cook and where do I make potions?

Both are in the Cauldron. On the Cook tab you pick up to 3 ingredients and the fire level: low fire is free, stronger fires cost Essence and make a better dish. Some mixes are secret recipes, which go to the Recipe Book. On the Alchemy tab you make potions and Tower items; each recipe shows what it needs, with what is missing in red. Finished dishes are kept in Care for your slime.

What are Orders?

The forest folk (Dona Corujilda, Chef Fungão, Fada Pirilu and Pipo) ask for potions, dishes and harvests. Open the Daily Missions and tap Orders: there are 3 a day, and each delivery pays Gold or Essence. Pipo sometimes brings a Special order with a bigger reward. Do not like an order? You can swap it for another one by paying Gold; each swap costs more until the next day.

Who is Pipo the Merchant and when does he show up?

Pipo visits the clearing twice a day, at 11 AM and 7 PM, and stays for a while (his shop shows how long until he leaves). He sells items, makes trades and also buys things from you for Gold; each offer has limited stock. If nothing interests you, the New offers button swaps everything for Gold (it costs more each time during the same visit). To be told when he arrives, keep the Travelling merchant notification on in Settings > Notifications.

What is the Selling Chest?

It is the chest with the coin sign, in the clearing. Tap it and put in the harvests, materials and dishes you have left over. When the day turns, everything inside becomes Gold. Until then, just tap an item in the chest to take it back. The chest pays less than Orders and Pipo, so use it for what you will not need.

What are the rare items from transmutations?

First you get collectibles that feed the Album (each only once). Once you have completed that fire's collectibles, the bonus starts giving crafting materials (such as Wood, Stone, Iron, Gold Ore and Diamond).

What do I do with materials (wood, stone, iron...)?

Materials are used to Craft cosmetics (Inventory, Craft tab) and to make potions and Tower items (Cauldron, Alchemy tab). Potions also ask for farm crops and cooked dishes. Each recipe shows what it needs and how much it costs.

What are potions for?

They are single-use consumables that give an effect for a limited time. You can have 3 effects at once: faster growth, a chance of double harvest and Touch of Gold (harvested Essence becomes Gold). Using another potion of the same kind replaces the active one, and the screen warns you first. Make potions at the Cauldron, on the Alchemy tab, and use them from the Consumables tab of the Inventory.

University

What is the Slime University?

It is the house where your slime studies. On the Study tab it learns skills: each study needs a level, Gold and time, and while studying the slime does not help on the farm. On the Skills tab you equip the ones you have learned (the number of slots grows with your level). Some studies are permanent and take no slot.

Which skills can the slime learn?

Automatic Harvest (harvests on its own), Automatic Planting (plants on its own), Agile Slime (walks, harvests and plants faster), Fertilizing Touch (chance to double the Essence from a harvest), Master Alchemist (cheaper Cauldron), Negotiator (cheaper seeds), Gardening (waters on its own) and Weeding (pulls weeds on its own). Permanent studies: Thrifty, Varied and Premium Planting (planting modes) and the larger watering can.

Items and Wardrobe

What is in the Inventory?

The Inventory has four tabs: Items (materials, collectibles and cosmetics), Consumables (potions and scrolls to use), Seeds and Craft (the cosmetic recipes).

How do I put a cosmetic on my slime?

Open the Wardrobe. Tapping an item shows a preview on the slime (even if you do not own it yet). If you own it, an Equip button appears; to take it off, use Remove. “Clear preview” goes back to what is really equipped. There are 7 slots: hat, face, neck, back, ground, left hand and right hand, one item per slot.

How do I change my slime's color?

In the Wardrobe, on the colors tab. Some colors are free and others come from the Store. Like with items, tapping a color shows a preview and the Apply button makes it official.

How do I take a photo of my slime?

In the Wardrobe, tap the camera button. Pick the face, the background, rotate the slime by dragging and use the zoom; then tap take photo. You can share it through your apps or save it to your gallery.

Can Store cosmetics also be crafted?

No. Items sold in the Store cannot be crafted, and craftable items are never sold. That way, free players reach the same place: the Store sells only style, never power.

Store and ads

Are ads mandatory? What does Remove Ads do?

No. The game never interrupts you with ads: they only show up if you choose to watch one, in exchange for a reward (collect double when you return, skip a plant's growth, retry a transmutation bonus, mini game bonus). With Remove Ads, the ad buttons disappear and the reward comes right away, with no video. The daily limits still apply.

How do I buy something in the Store?

Open the Store and choose from the Packs & Services, Equippables or Seasonal tabs. Items your slime wears go through the fitting room first, so you can see how they look. Purchases are made through Google Play. Each item is bought once, stays yours forever and then shows as Owned.

What is the Starter Pack?

A welcome offer you can buy only once: 10,000 Gold, 100 Tatopo seeds and the Pioneer's Cloak, an exclusive cosmetic. It is available for 48 hours, and the Store shows how much time is left.

Friends and Ranking

How do I add friends?

Open Friends and go to Add. Type your friend's code and send the request. Your own code is on the same screen, with buttons to copy and share it. The request shows up in your friend's Requests tab and once they accept, you appear in each other's list.

What can I do with friends?

Visit your friend's farm, send a gift (one per friend per day) and see the Ranking. Gifts you receive wait in an inbox, and the Friends button glows when there is a request or gift waiting.

How do I visit a friend's farm and how do gifts work?

In the Friends list, tap Visit to see your friend's farm and the slime they are using, and Gift to send a present (one per friend per day). Gifts sent to you wait in the gift box: the button lights up and you claim them whenever you like, up to 10 per day.

How does the Ranking work?

The Ranking compares you only with your friends, in four tabs: Level, Gold, Essence and Tower (the highest floor of the Infinite Tower). Gold and Essence are the current balance, so spending lowers your position. Use the refresh button for the newest data and hide a friend if you want them off the list.

Mini games

What are the mini games and what do I get for playing?

There are four: Memory, Sky Catch, Match Three and Bubble Pop (plus the Infinite Tower, which has its own guide). Each round gives up to 3 stars and pays Essence. The prize is proportional to the time you actually played, so playing for real pays more than losing on purpose. There is no daily limit.

Tips for getting 3 stars in the mini games Tip

Memory: try to remember where the cards you have already seen are, and flip new cards before repeating. Sky Catch: drag the slime to catch everything and dodge the rocks (each costs a life); catching in a row raises the combo. Match Three: look for moves that start cascades, and if you stay still for a few seconds the game blinks a possible swap. Bubble Pop: aim using the walls, drop whole groups of loose bubbles and watch the red line.

Infinite Tower

What is the Infinite Tower?

A tower with endless floors. On each floor your slime takes a turn-based challenge against a cute rival: nobody gets hurt, the rival naps when it loses and your slime gets sleepy if its Vigor runs out. Losing never costs you a floor and you can try again. The tower always opens at the highest floor you reached. Every 10 floors there is a Guardian.

What kinds of floors does the Tower have?

Normal floor: a regular challenge. Special rival: stronger, pays better. Guardian: the boss every 10 floors. Treasure: a chest to open, no fight. Rest stop: your slime takes a nap before the next challenge. Merchant: a little shop with useful items. Friend challenge: a friend shows up as your rival. Treasure, rest and merchant only pay on the first visit.

How do I win the Tower challenges?

Each turn the rival announces what it will do. You choose Strike (attack), Protect (defend) or Skill (spends the 3 charge stars). Answering the warning correctly (Strike against a charging rival, Protect against an attack) charges your skill faster. The stronger your slime, the better: your level and the 7 accessory slots count, and you can Upgrade each slot with Gold, Essence and fragments. Active effects show next to the slime and the rival with the turns left, and the History button shows what happened each turn.

Tips for climbing higher in the Tower Tip

Wear an accessory in all 7 slots (an empty slot helps nothing) and upgrade slots little by little; a slot's level stays even if you swap the item. Guardians are the walls: if you get stuck, upgrade and come back. Floors you already beat pay little, so the big gains come from new floors. Bring the skill that fits the rival: protection against hard hitters, offense against rivals with lots of Vigor.

What are the Tower skills?

You take one per challenge and it spends the 3 charge stars. Giant Strike: a much stronger strike. Gel Wall: creates a shield. Green Heal: restores Vigor. Shine: the rival misses its strikes for a few turns. Spore Cloud: a chance for the rival to fall asleep and lose turns. Duplicate: your next strike hits several times. Leaf Storm: one hit now and more gusts on the next turns. Dew: heals a little and gives a shield. The Hat boosts the power of all of them.

What are the Tower items for?

Using one spends your turn, so pick the moment. Heal Potion restores Vigor; Strength Tonic makes your strikes stronger for a few turns; Protective Shell gives a shield; Sleepy Dust makes the rival lose its next turn; Revive Feather wakes you up if you doze off; Focus Crystal makes your Skill ready at once; Tickle Bomb takes a bit of the rival's Vigor and makes it lose a turn; the Map Scroll shows the traits of upcoming floors. They come from treasures, the Tower merchant and the Cauldron's Alchemy tab, and you can carry at most 9 of each.

What does each accessory slot improve in the Tower?

Each slot improves a different stat: Hat = Skill Power, Face = Lucky strike, Neck = Vigor, Back = Resistance, Ground = Regeneration, Left hand = Starting shield, Right hand = Strength. Your level also raises Vigor, Strength and Resistance. A slot opens once you own at least one accessory for it (no need to wear it), and the item's rarity gives a head start.

What does Upgrade mean and what do I spend?

Upgrading raises a slot's level and improves its stat. It costs Gold and, later on, also Essence and Tower fragments (which drop from special rivals, Guardians and treasure). Each level costs more than the last. The level belongs to the slot, so it stays even if you swap the item.

How do I unlock new Tower skills and switch the one I use?

You unlock each skill by beating certain Tower floors (the map screen shows which floor brings the next one). Giant Strike is unlocked from the start. To switch, use the arrows on the skill bar at the bottom of the tower map.

Seasons and Album

How do seasons work?

There are 8 per year (such as Christmas, Halloween, Carnival and Easter), repeating every year, and the screen shows the dates. During a season you earn free Fragments and spend them in the Craft tab to make the event's exclusive items. The check-in gives fragments per day and keeps up to 3 days if you do not log in; missions pay once each, and there is a bonus for playing on 7 different days. The Seasonal Store only sells during the event. When the season ends, leftover fragments turn into Gold. Items are yours forever and if you missed one you can get it next time.

What is the Album?

It keeps your slime's story: 50 memories in 5 chapters. Each Memory Fragment you find reveals a new memory; they can drop as a bonus from Cauldron transmutations and never come repeated. There is also an Extra chapter, Life in the Clearing, with memories outside the main story. The Album also holds the collection of what you have found.

Account and help

Can I play without an account?

Yes. At the start you can choose Continue without account, and your progress stays saved only on this device. It is worth linking your Google account later to get a cloud backup.

How do I link my Google account?

In Settings > Profile, tap Link Google Account. This backs up your farm to the cloud and lets you recover your progress on another device. If that Google account already has a save, the game shows both (the account one and this device's) and you choose which to keep. Nothing is deleted before you choose.

How do I save to the cloud now and check that it worked?

In Settings, the cloud line shows the status (synced, device only, no connection) and the time of the last backup. Tap Save to cloud now to force a backup.

Is my progress saved? Can I play on another device?

The game saves on the device by itself, all the time. To lose nothing when changing phones, link your Google account in Settings > Profile: that keeps a cloud backup, which you recover on the new device by signing in with the same account. Avoid playing on two devices at the same time, because one can overwrite the other's progress.

It says my account is open on another device. What should I do?

The warning shows when the game notices the same account was used a moment ago on another device. Playing on both at once can erase one device's progress in the cloud. Continue here: this device becomes the one that keeps the progress. Use the cloud one: replaces this device's save with the cloud one (a copy of what was here is kept). If you only have one device, choose Continue here.

I see “No connection”. Do I lose any progress?

No. Your progress is saved on your device. Only the parts that need internet (Friends, Ranking, Store and ads) are unavailable until the connection returns.

How do I change the language or turn off notifications?

In Settings. There you change the language (Portuguese or English), adjust sound and graphics, and choose which notifications you want: harvest, study complete, potions, seasons, absence reminders, slime care and travelling merchant.

Which notifications does the game send?

Seven kinds: harvest ready, study complete, potions running out, seasons (when one starts and the day before it ends), absence reminders after a few days without playing, slime care (hunger, affection and sleep) and travelling merchant (when Pipo arrives). You turn each kind on or off in Settings > Notifications. Season and absence notices only arrive during the day.

The game asked me to allow "Alarms & reminders". What is it for?

It lets notifications arrive right on time (for example, when Pipo returns or the harvest is ready). Without this permission Android may delay notices to save battery. It is optional: tap Not now if you prefer, and the game works normally.

Can I lock day or night in the game?

Yes. In Settings, the Time of day option has three modes: Real time (follows your device clock), Always day and Always night. The sunrise and sunset achievements only count in Real time mode.

The game feels heavy or slow on my phone. What can I do?

In Settings > Graphics, pick a lower quality. You can also turn on the FPS counter to keep track and adjust the UI size. Close other apps and, if it is still bad, send a report through Support mentioning your phone model.

How do I change my name?

In Settings > Profile, type the new name and tap Save. Names go through an inappropriate-words filter.

What are titles and how do I equip one?

Titles are nicknames you unlock with achievements. Open Titles, pick an unlocked one and tap Equip. The equipped title shows next to your name, including to your friends.

What are achievements and what do I get from them?

They are game goals, split into nine tabs: Economy & Wealth, Botany & Harvest, Cauldron Mastery, The Scholar, The Collector & Secrets, Mini Games, Infinite Tower, Gardening and Care. When you complete one you get a reward (XP, Gold or items) and may unlock titles. Some are secret and show as ??? until you find them.

Where can I see my statistics?

In Settings, open Statistics. It shows play time, days played, Gold and Essence earned and spent, plants harvested, transmutations done, items crafted, Daily Missions completed and more. Rows with a star count toward achievements.

How do I contact support or report a problem?

In Settings > Support. Describe what happened (what you were doing and what you expected), copy the support code or the automatic report and send it by email. The Open Email button prepares the message for you. The game version is shown on the same screen.

How do I get a copy of my data?

In Settings > Privacy and data, tap Download my data and then Copy my data. Your data goes to the clipboard and you can paste it into a text file or an email to keep a copy.

How do I delete my account and data?

In Settings > Privacy and data, tap Delete my account. The game asks for two confirmations because it permanently erases everything (farm, purchases and friends) and cannot be undone. You need to be connected to the internet.

Support and problems

How do I report a problem or a bug?

Open Settings > Support. Describe what happened (what you were doing, what you expected and what you saw), tap Copy Code or Copy report and send it by email to rigamurastudios@gmail.com. The Open Email button builds the message with your support code. The code and report help us find your case without you having to explain everything again.

What information should I send to support? Tip

The more complete, the faster we can fix it: 1) the support code (Settings > Support); 2) what happened and what you were doing; 3) on which screen and roughly what time; 4) your phone model and Android version; 5) the game version (shown on the Support screen); 6) a screenshot or short video, if you have one. For purchases, also include the Google Play order number.

The game froze or closed on its own. What should I do?

Open the game again: it saves your progress all the time, so you should not lose anything. Make sure you have the latest version from Google Play. If it happens again, send a report through Support telling us what you were doing at that moment (for example, opening the Tower or taking a photo) and your phone model.

I paid for an item but it did not show up. What should I do?

First, wait a minute with the internet on and reopen the game: on startup it checks your purchases with Google Play and delivers anything that is missing. Check in the Store whether the item shows as Owned and in the Wardrobe. If it did not show up, send Support: your support code, the item name and the order number (it is in the Google Play receipt email, something like GPA.xxxx). With that we check the purchase and deliver the item.

I got "Purchase failed". What should I do?

Check that your internet works, that you are signed in to Google Play and that your payment method is valid. Close and reopen the game and try again. If the message stays, send a report through Support telling us which item you tried to buy. If a charge shows up on your card despite the failure, see the question about a paid item that did not show up.

I was charged twice or the amount was wrong. What should I do?

Payments are processed by Google Play, so check your Google Play order history: each charge shows there with its order number. Each item can only be bought once per account, so two charges are usually for different items or different accounts. Send the order number to Support along with your code and we will look into what happened. To dispute a card charge, the route is through Google Play and your card issuer.

I changed phones. Do I lose what I bought?

No. Purchases are tied to your Google Play account and each item is bought only once. On the new phone, sign in to the same Google Play account, install the game and link your Google account in Settings > Profile to recover your progress. On startup the game also checks your purchases with Google Play and gives back anything missing. If a purchased item does not show up, contact Support with the order number.

How do I ask for a refund or chargeback of a purchase?

The purchase is made through Google Play, so the refund request goes through it: open your Google Play account order history, pick the purchase and use the refund or report-a-problem option. The rules and deadlines are Google Play's. You can also write to us through Support with the order number and we will help follow up.

I lost my progress. What should I do?

Do not uninstall the game or clear the app data before talking to us. Check in Settings whether the cloud shows “synced” and the date of the last backup: if you linked your Google account, your progress may be recoverable. Contact Support with your code and tell us when you noticed the loss and whether you changed phones or reinstalled.

A gift or a reward did not arrive. What should I do?

Friends' gifts wait in the gift inbox: the Friends button glows and you claim them there. If it is not there, refresh the list and make sure both you and your friend are on the latest version and online. Mini game, Tower and achievement rewards are paid instantly; if one did not come, send your support code and tell us which one and when.

A player is bothering me or has an offensive name. How do I report them?

You only interact with people you added as friends. To step away, remove the person from your Friends list: you stop seeing each other's farms and trading gifts. To report them, send Support the person's name and tell us what happened.

The ad will not load or I did not get the reward. What should I do?

Check your internet and try again in a bit: an ad is not always available right away. Watch it to the end, because closing early gives no reward. Each feature has a daily ad limit, and the screen shows when it comes back. If you watched it fully and got nothing, send your support code and say which feature it was.

I see “Your account has social features and the Store blocked”. What is this?

It means your account was restricted: Friends, Ranking, gifts and purchases are unavailable, but you can still play your farm normally. If you think it was a mistake, contact support in Settings > Support with your support code.
